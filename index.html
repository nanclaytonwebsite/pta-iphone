Apple Calendar Users: Subscribe Here

Subscribe to the Nan Clayton PTA Calendar to receive event updates.

What to Expect

1
The subscription link button below will ask to open Apple Calendar.
2
"Add Subscription Calendar" will auto populate the subscription URL window.
3
Once auto populated, select Find.
4
Title will read Nan Clayton PTA Event Calendar.
5
Once auto populated, click the check mark in the upper right corner to accept.
6
Adjust calendar settings (color and reminders) and then close the options (X in the upper left corner).
Nan Clayton PTA Calendar - Subscribe
Be sure to "subscribe" as a new calendar - note importing events to an existing apple calendar may not refresh.
Subscriptions stay connected to the PTA calendar so future changes can be received automatically!
